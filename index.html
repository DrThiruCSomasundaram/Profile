★ Software Launched - Kjeldahl Nitrogen Factor Calculator

A web application for calculating the Kjeldahl nitrogen factor using amino acid content and total Kjeldahl nitrogen of food or feed products. Developed at Deakin University.

★ Software Launched - ISO Protein Energy diet formulation

★ Software Launched - ISO Protein Energy diet formulation

An interactive web-based calculator for formulating and optimizing experimental feed compositions. This tool helps researchers and nutritionists create iso-protein and iso-energetic experimental diets.

ICoP: Rome, Italy. Writing workshop
